## Third-party anatomical bone meshes (ArtiSynth / dynjaw)

Initial anatomical bone mesh files were obtained from the dynjaw demo of the
ArtiSynth framework ([https://www.artisynth.org/Software/ModelsDownload](https://www.artisynth.org/Software/ModelsDownload)).

  Hannam, A. G., Stavness, I., Lloyd, J. E., & Fels, S. (2008).
  A dynamic model of jaw and hyoid biomechanics during chewing.
  Journal of Biomechanics, 41(5), 1069–1076.
